03 / THE BUSINESS RELATIONSHIP

A CLEAR COMMERCIAL ARRANGEMENT

You commission the creative work.
You decide how to use the book.

CICAN develops the agreed photography, illustrations, book design and usage rights. The client normally arranges and funds printing and distribution. Production management can be agreed separately. Scope, fees, approvals, deadlines and licensing are confirmed before commissioned work begins.

05 / THE CICAN METHOD

Clear steps.
Permission first.

01

Discover

Understand the business, its audience and the visual details worth capturing.

02

Request permission

Agree access, photography boundaries and intended use with the right representative.

03

Photograph

Capture approved scenes and details, avoiding identifiable people by default.

04

Illustrate

Transform selected photographs into original, print-ready line art.

05

Present

Share a private visual proposal and invite the business's feedback.

06

Agree & deliver

Confirm the commission, rights, fees and production plan before final delivery.

Respect comes first. CICAN does not assume permission to enter premises, photograph private property, reproduce protected branding or publish a private demonstration. Public portfolio use requires separate approval.
