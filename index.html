01 / THE CONCEPT

From places
to possibilities.

We go beyond the storefront, the product and the first impression.

We visit real business environments, observe the details that make them distinctive and capture them through original photography. Selected images are then reimagined as refined black-and-white line art — ready to become a bespoke publication, creative campaign or new brand experience.

The result is more than an illustration. It is a different way for people to explore, remember and engage with a business.

05 / ABOUT THE FOUNDER

A different way
of looking closer.

Founded by CICAN GEORGE ALIN, CICAN ART OF BUSINESS is built around a simple belief: the most interesting brand stories are often hiding in plain sight.

The founder's physical presence is central to the approach. By being there, walking through a space and noticing its details first-hand, the work starts with direct observation rather than a generic visual brief.

This independent creative consultancy brings together discovery, photography, illustration and commercial thinking to help organisations explore new ways of presenting their worlds.
