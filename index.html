01 / THE CONCEPT

Real places.
New possibilities.

We look at a business and see more than its products, premises or storefront.

We discover the visual details that make each place distinctive. With the business's permission, we photograph selected areas, transform chosen images into original black-and-white line art and develop a tailored publication concept.

The flagship idea is a bespoke colouring book that a business may choose to make available to visiting families — a creative take-home experience inspired by the place they have just visited.

Every proposal is individual. No two businesses need the same story, illustrations or publication.

04 / THE CICAN METHOD

Discover. Create.
Connect.

A permission-led process that turns an individual business environment into a private creative proposal and, where commissioned, a finished publication.

01

Discover

Identify a business and understand its environment and potential visual story.

02

Request permission

Contact the appropriate representative and agree access, photography boundaries and intended use before the visit.

03

Photograph

Capture approved subjects, avoiding identifiable people and personal information by default.

04

Transform

Create a small selection of original black-and-white illustrations from approved photographs.

05

Present

Send a personalised, private visual proposal to the relevant contact or head office and invite feedback.

06

Agree & deliver

If the business is interested, agree scope, fees, rights, approvals and production responsibilities before completing the commission.

Permission comes first. CICAN does not assume a right to enter premises, photograph property, reproduce protected branding or publish a private demonstration. Public portfolio use requires separate approval.

05 / ABOUT THE FOUNDER

A different way
of looking closer.

CICAN ART OF BUSINESS was founded by CICAN GEORGE ALIN and is built around a simple belief: every business has a visual world worth discovering.

The work begins with first-hand observation. By visiting a place with permission, noticing its details and capturing original photographs, the creative process starts with the real environment rather than a generic template.

CICAN brings together visual discovery, photography, illustration and publication design to create individual concepts for organisations.
